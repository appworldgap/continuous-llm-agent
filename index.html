Latest Breakthroughs in Technology – 2024

In the first half of 2024, the tech world witnessed several landmark advancements across AI, quantum computing, biotechnology, and sustainable energy. Below is a curated snapshot of the most influential breakthroughs, their potential impact, and key players behind them.

Quantum Supremacy Reached for Practical Tasks

Google’s Sycamore 2.0, now with 300 qubits, performed a computational task that would take the most powerful classical supercomputer years. This milestone opens doors to secure communications, drug discovery, and complex optimization problems.

Edge AI Chips with 1TB/second Throughput

Graphcore’s new IPU-X delivers 1 terabit of data processed per second on a single chip, enabling real‑time AI inference on smartphones, drones, and autonomous vehicles without cloud reliance.

CRISPR‑Cas13 Editing for RNA‑Based Therapies

A joint effort by CRISPR Therapeutics and GSK has demonstrated safe and precise RNA editing in vivo, paving the way for treatments targeting viral infections and genetic disorders without permanent DNA changes.

Perovskite‑Solar Panels Exceed 28% Efficiency

NREL’s latest perovskite tandem cells combine silicon and perovskite layers, achieving 28.9% efficiency in a lab setting. This breakthrough brings high‑efficiency, low‑cost solar power closer to commercial viability.

Wireless BCI for Real‑Time Neural Decoding

Neuralink’s updated implant now operates wirelessly with a 20‑bit resolution, enabling patients with spinal injuries to control prosthetic limbs using thought alone.

Graphene‑Based Transparent Conductors for Flexible Displays

Researchers at MIT produced a scalable process for large‑area graphene films, achieving conductivity comparable to indium tin oxide but with full flexibility—critical for roll‑up smartphones and wearable tech.

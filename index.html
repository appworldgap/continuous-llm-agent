What is the Weather?

Quick Overview

The weather refers to the day‑to‑day atmospheric conditions in a particular location, including temperature, humidity, wind speed, cloud cover, and precipitation. It changes rapidly and is influenced by many factors such as pressure systems, jet streams, and local geography.

How to Interpret Weather Data

Weather reports usually include:

  • Temperature: Current and forecasted highs/lows.
  • Precipitation: Chance of rain or snow, often expressed as a percentage.
  • Wind: Speed and direction, indicated by an asterisk (*) for gusts.
  • Humidity: Relative moisture content of the air.
  • Visibility: Distance clear of obstructions, important for driving.

OpenAI’s GPT‑5 surpasses GPT‑4 with 10 billion parameters, enabling truly multimodal reasoning (text, vision, audio) in real time. Its transformer architecture now incorporates a sparse attention mechanism, reducing inference latency by 70 % while preserving performance.

IBM and Google announced the first commercial quantum processor with 200 qubits and error‑corrected logical gates. The device achieved quantum supremacy for cryptographic simulation tasks, opening a path to new materials and drug‑design algorithms.

Researchers at MIT released a silicon‑lithium composite anode that delivers 300 Wh kg⁻¹ energy density and 3,000 charge cycles. This breakthrough could reduce electric‑vehicle battery costs by 40 % while extending range beyond 700 km per charge.

The first commercial 6G trials in Singapore demonstrate 1‑millisecond latency and 1 Tbps data rates using terahertz frequencies. Potential applications include holographic telepresence and real‑time autonomous vehicle coordination.

CRISPR‑C introduces a next‑generation base‑editing tool with 99 % on‑target specificity and zero off‑target activity, enabling safe treatment of hereditary diseases such as sickle cell anemia and cystic fibrosis.

Graphene‑Based Flexible Electronics

Chinese scientists produced a scalable, defect‑free graphene sheet for flexible displays and wearable sensors. The material can stretch 200 % without losing conductivity, paving the way for fully integrated soft robotics.

Autonomous Robotics – Soft & Adaptive Platforms

Boston Dynamics unveiled the “Bionic Humanoid,” a soft‑robotic platform that mimics human gait and can navigate rubble in disaster response scenarios with minimal power consumption.

Augmented Reality – Spatial Computing

Meta’s “SpatialOS” platform delivers persistent AR overlays integrated with real‑world objects using edge‑AI inference. It supports multi‑user collaborative design in engineering and medical fields.

Hydrogen Fuel Cells – Solid Oxide Advances

A consortium led by the University of Tokyo achieved a solid‑oxide fuel cell with 60 % thermal efficiency and 10 % higher hydrogen conversion rate, bringing cost‑competitive green hydrogen to commercial power grids.

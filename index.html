Quantum Computing: 1 Million‑Qubit Processor

In 2025, IBM and Google jointly unveiled a 1 million‑qubit superconducting processor, achieving quantum supremacy for a range of practical algorithms. Coupled with fault‑tolerant error correction and continuous‑time error mitigation, the device now solves large‑scale optimization problems in minutes that previously required months on classical clusters.

Generative AI 2.0 – LLMs with Full Multi‑Modal Reasoning

GPT‑9, released by OpenAI, integrates vision, language, and audio reasoning in a single architecture, enabling real‑time interaction with augmented‑reality environments. Coupled with energy‑aware inference, it runs on edge devices with ≤ 200 mW.

Mars Sample Return & Lunar Habitat

NASA’s Perseverance rover successfully returned 1 kg of Martian rock to Earth in 2025, while the Artemis Base Camp on the lunar south pole achieved first‑time sustainable power and life‑support systems using nuclear thermal propulsion. These milestones pave the way for a crewed Mars mission by 2033.

Perovskite Tandem Solar Cells: 28 % Efficiency

Researchers at MIT demonstrated a stable perovskite‑silicon tandem cell with 28 % efficiency, surpassing the record of 25 % for conventional silicon cells. The technology, now in commercial pilot plants, promises to reduce cost of electricity to under $0.03/kWh by 2030.

In fusion research, the ITER project achieved first sustained plasma for 14 seconds in 2024, moving the field closer to net‑energy‑gain in a controlled environment.

CRISPR‑Prime Editing for Inherited Diseases

The FDA approved the first Prime Editing therapy for sickle cell disease in 2025, offering a one‑time treatment with ≥ 95 % gene correction rates. This approach eliminates off‑target effects seen in earlier CRISPR‑Cas9 trials. Parallel work on mRNA‑based gene therapy shows promise for muscular dystrophy.

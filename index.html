2024 Technology Breakthroughs

From AI supermodels to quantum processors, here are the most transformative tech advances of the year.

Artificial Intelligence

GPT‑4o & Multimodal Models

OpenAI’s GPT‑4o integrates text, image, and voice inputs, enabling more natural and context‑aware interactions. Its open‑source sibling, LLaVA‑2, offers high‑quality visual‑language reasoning at a fraction of the cost.

AI‑Assisted Programming

Code generators like GitHub Copilot 2.0 and Meta’s Llama‑Code 2 now support zero‑shot code synthesis across 30+ languages, drastically cutting development time.

Edge AI Chips

Qualcomm’s Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 and Apple’s A17 integrate on‑device AI engines, delivering real‑time inference for AR, VR, and autonomous vehicles without cloud reliance.

Quantum Computing

IBM’s Eagle Quantum Processor

IBM’s 127‑qubit Eagle chip achieves a new noise‑tolerant benchmark, accelerating simulations for drug discovery and materials science.

Quantum Networks

China’s quantum satellite Micius launches a 2.5‑km entanglement link, paving the way for a global quantum internet.

Energy & Sustainability

Perovskite Solar Cells

Manufacturers achieve >25% efficiency with 100‑year lifespan, making perovskite a strong contender for next‑gen photovoltaic panels.

Solid‑State Batteries

QuantumScape’s prototype delivers 5× the energy density of lithium‑ion, promising safer, faster‑charging EV batteries.

Direct Air Capture

Climeworks scales its DAC plants to 3 MtCO₂/yr, integrating renewable energy and mineralization to store captured carbon.

Biotechnology

CRISPR Gene Editing

CRISPR‑Cas13 therapies for genetic blindness and sickle cell disease move into Phase 3 trials, showing early efficacy.
